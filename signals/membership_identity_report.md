- SHLD: The 2016 annual report identifies Sears common stock as SHLD. This is an issuer observation, not an all-dates security mapping. [Source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1310067/000131006717000005/shld201610k.htm).
- SHLD: A separate ETF listed using SHLD in September 2023. Its prices cannot be assigned to Sears shares. [Source](https://www.globalxetfs.com/articles/introducing-shld-the-case-for-defense-tech).
- BBBY: The 2022 annual report associates BBBY common stock with the original issuer; the CIK identifies the issuer, not every share class. [Source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/886158/000088615822000047/bbby-20220226.htm).
- BBBY: Beyond adopted BBBY effective 2025-08-29; this is a different issuer from the old BBBY company. [Source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1130713/000114036125032363/ef20054350_8k.htm).
- FOXA: The old issuer renamed its shares TFCFA/TFCF during the 2019 separation while the new Fox traded as FOXA/FOX. A continuous FOXA ticker interval does not establish security continuity. [Source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1308161/000119312519079034/d721340d8k.htm).
- FOXA: New Fox began standalone regular trading on 2019-03-19 following a share distribution. Source prices require separate security and distribution accounting. [Source](https://www.foxcorporation.com/news/corp-press-releases/2019/fox-corporation-announces-first-day-as-standalone/).
- IR: The former Ingersoll-Rand plc became Trane Technologies and traded as TT on 2020-03-02; its industrial segment combined with Gardner Denver. A continuous IR interval spans a material identity transition. [Source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1466258/000114036120004470/nc10009348x1_ex99-1.htm).
- IR: The post-transaction IR issuer has CIK 0001699150, separate from the former Ingersoll-Rand plc filer. These CIK observations still do not certify raw share-class history. [Source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1699150/000114036120004816/form8k.htm).

Candidate history ends 2017-07-24; trading availability and index membership must be reconciled separately.
